# GWEC Global Wind Report 2025 (excerpt)

--- page 1 ---
Headline numbers. 117 GW of new wind capacity was installed in 2024, a record year.
Cumulative global wind capacity passed 1,136 GW.

--- page 2 ---
Offshore. Offshore wind added 11 GW in 2024. Floating offshore pilots in Norway, the UK and
Japan demonstrated turbines in water deeper than 100 m.

--- page 3 ---
Risks. Permitting delays and supply-chain bottlenecks for nacelles and blades are the top two
risks to meeting 2030 targets. Average permitting time in Europe exceeds 5 years.
