# IEA Solar PV Outlook 2025 (excerpt)

--- page 1 ---
Executive summary. Global solar PV additions reached 597 GW in 2024, a 33% increase on 2023.
Solar now accounts for over three quarters of all new renewable capacity.

--- page 2 ---
Costs. Average module prices fell to $0.09/W in late 2024. Utility-scale LCOE is now below
$0.04/kWh in most sunbelt markets, making solar the cheapest source of new electricity.

--- page 3 ---
Constraints. Grid connection queues and curtailment are now the binding constraints in China,
Europe and the United States. Curtailment in some regions exceeded 10% of generation.

--- page 4 ---
Outlook. The IEA base case projects 4,000 GW of cumulative installed PV capacity by 2030.
